Install on Windows

Use Windows 10 or 11 on an Intel or AMD 64-bit PC with a current Edge or Chrome browser. Connect to the internet for the first installation.

  1. Choose Download for Windows and open En-GYM-Setup.exe.
  2. Let setup finish downloading the English voices and speech recognizer. If the Microsoft Visual C++ Runtime is needed, Windows may ask permission to install it.
  3. Open En-GYM from the desktop or Start menu. It opens in your browser. In Settings → Microphone and sound, choose Check microphone and allow access.

The first installer has no Windows publisher certificate. Windows may display an unknown-publisher warning or block it under your device policy. Use the official download and keep Windows security protections enabled.

Your first practice

Choose Try Listen and Repeat for a short microphone session. Play the prompt, repeat it aloud, and finish your recording. You can replay your own answer.

For a timed practice test for the TOEFL iBT® test, choose Start a full mock. Under Practice, choose a section or a specific task. Availability reflects the prepared material on this computer.

Review an attempt

Open History and select an attempt. The report lets you move between responses, revisit the original prompt and replay saved recordings. Objective questions are checked locally.

With AI connected, use Grade writing or Grade speaking for submitted responses that need grading. Discuss results starts a conversation about the attempt. Score ranges are estimates.

Build your vocabulary

Highlight a word or phrase in an exercise, its report, a vocabulary text or a game. Choose Save to vocabulary. In Vocabulary → Games, choose a round, a timed round or endless practice. Select words and use Write a text to get a reading passage on your chosen topic.

AI and local data

Test history, microphone recordings and vocabulary are stored on your PC. Phonon reads prompts locally, and Whisper transcribes your speech locally. AI features send relevant text, transcripts and measurements to the provider selected in Settings → AI trainer.

Choose separate models for grading and chat, creating exercises, and reviewing them. Automatic preparation can use the provider’s allowance while En-GYM is running, including after closing the browser. Switch it off in Settings or choose Quit in the tray to stop the app.

Update En-GYM or its speech tools

Open Settings → Updates → Check for updates. Select the application, voices or speech recognition, review the download sizes, then choose Update and restart. Finish any active exam first. Your scores and transcripts stay in History.

Uninstalling En-GYM removes the program and its shortcuts. Windows keeps your practice history, recordings and downloaded speech components in your local En-GYM folder for a future installation.
